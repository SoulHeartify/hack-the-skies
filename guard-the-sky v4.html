GUARD THE SKY.

You're the air traffic controller at a busy, sustainability-focused airport. Keep aircraft separated, clear arrivals and departures, and make every second count.

  • Complete 10 rounds per shift, across 5 shifts — case doesn't matter.
  • After START, press ▶ PLAY to begin each shift. Upgrade your systems first if you like.
  • On the radar, two aircraft close in every round. Type the instruction before they collide.
  • You get 2 warnings per shift. Two collisions ends the shift — you'll restart it with the credits and upgrades you had at its start. Progress from earlier shifts is kept.
  • Upgrades cap at level 15, and each level costs more than the last. After shift 5, clock out for your overall report.
