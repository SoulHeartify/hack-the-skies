You're the air traffic controller at a busy, sustainability-focused airport. Keep aircraft separated, clear arrivals and departures, and make every second count.

  • Complete 10 rounds per shift, across 5 shifts — case doesn't matter.

  • On the radar, the two aircraft close in every round. Type the command before they collide.
  • You get 2 warnings per shift. Two collisions ends the shift early — credits and upgrades carry over.
